Preface

This is Trivium: Ancient Study for Modern Writers, an open-source reference book I’m writing. My thesis is this: the best tutor of creative writing is the trivium. The writer’s craft requires three things: reading, thinking, and writing. Grammar teahces you how to read, Logic teaches you how to think, and Rhetoric teaches you how to write.
