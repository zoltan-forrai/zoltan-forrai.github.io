Preface

This is Trivium: Ancient Study for Modern Writers, an open-source reference book I’m preparing to write. Well, book might be a strong descriptor; it’s more a collection of loose notes and lousy ideas, organised according to my own fancy. The sincere purpose of this labour is to help budding writers (me) achieve mastery over their craft. My thesis is this: the best tutor of creative writing is the trivium.

The page is live; each addition, deletion, and revision is published (almost) right away. This means the book is presented as is, mid-draft or otherwise. The writing continues as I learn more about my subject. Therefore, some parts of the text may remain fragmentary for a while till I wrap my head around its topic.
