Welcome to My Weblog?

The internet is dead, and it's a gruesome crime scene. The coroner's report is quite clear; the web was killed. Who done it? We all did.

The online creator sphere is in a fragile state right now. The once working contract between platforms and creators is breaking, dissolving at the seams. This contract once was simple: users only visited sites if there was content to be seen, so platforms needed creators to pull those users in, and in return the creator was rewarded with some of the revenue the users generated. The creator provided the content, the platform provided the infrastructure. It was the deal many people built careers upon.

This deal is no longer desirable for platforms. Creativity is unreliable, it's messy, it's altogether too human. And when your singular goal is profit, humanity becomes a liability. Just imagine how much revenue they could save, if only they didn't need creatives to make content. Well, enter Ai...

I'm not on social media anymore, the malefits simply outweigh the benefits. They are all too eager to replace human creativity with artificial crap. But all Ai has done so far was pollute the web, and turn the internet into a reeking landfill. Human creativity is no longer welcomed, let alone desired. The online creator ladder is now encrusted in shit. And no matter how I look at it, social media looks like a sinking ship.

The only platform, I believe, exempt from this critique is Newgrounds. With 30 years of history, Newgrounds stands as an exemplary case against web corporatisation: an artist focused architecture, no evil algorithm, a strong and committed community, and a firm stance against gen Ai. While I don't wish NG to become mainstream (that's the worst thing that can happen to a niche), I do see a future where Newgrounds becomes a big player on the web.

Time moves like a pendulum, it swings, forth and back between extremes, never quite reaching equilibrium.1. If that is so, then the present's push towards the artificial will be mirrored by an equal swing, back to being human.
