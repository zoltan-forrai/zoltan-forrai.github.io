Who am I?

This site exists to help me answer this question. Online I go by Gildrom, but my real name is Zoltán, Zoli for short. I’m a quarter of a century old. I call Europe my home.

In high school, I studied media and filmmaking, but I was left unsure whether to continue with it. I spent one semester in film school at a reasonably good university, but after much disappointment, I left and pivoted careers.

My other love, besides media, is the English language 🇬🇧, so naturally, I turned to language teaching as a viable path forward. So now I study English and history at a more distinguished, albeit much more demanding university.

Why Do I Write?

To better my English. While I subscribe to Krashen’s input hypothesis, I also realise that simply relying on natural input available through everyday life and media can only get you so far. To go further you must seek more strenuous, though rarely as compelling texts. I supplement this reading practice with a practice of writing.

I also cannot deny that I find writing (the ideation, the tactile feeling of pressing keys, the act of creation) tremendously enjoyable. I approach language as a game, writing as play, and my preeminent priority is ever my own entertainment.
